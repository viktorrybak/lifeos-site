LifeOS

A private personal assistant system of Viktor Rybak.

LifeOS is a single-user personal system. It helps its owner keep tasks, notes, reminders and calendar in one place and work with his own Google accounts (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Forms, Tasks, Contacts, Chat) through a private assistant.

It is not a public service: no one except the owner can sign in or use it.

Privacy policy · Contact: viktor.rybak@gmail.com
